# SEO Off-Page Playbook

This repository can improve technical and on-page SEO directly. Off-page SEO still needs ongoing work outside the codebase.

## Priority Work

1. Create and maintain verified business profiles for the brand on major platforms.
2. Build healthcare-directory citations with consistent `Name / Address / Phone / Website` details.
3. Earn backlinks from trusted local organizations, hospitals, clinics, health bloggers, and city resource pages.
4. Publish link-worthy content clusters around symptoms, departments, medicine education, and city-based healthcare discovery.
5. Request links from hospitals, doctors, wellness partners, and article contributors where appropriate.
6. Track branded mentions and convert unlinked mentions into backlinks.

## Citation Consistency

- Keep the same brand spelling everywhere: `Arogio`.
- Use the same primary domain and canonical HTTPS URL on every listing.
- Keep contact emails, support details, and social profile links consistent.
- Update old listings whenever phone numbers, city focus, or branding changes.

## Content Promotion

- Share new article pages with short summaries on social channels.
- Send newly published articles to healthcare communities and newsletter lists.
- Re-promote evergreen pages every few months after factual refreshes.
- Prioritize pages with strong search intent: doctors, hospitals, blood banks, medicines, and symptom education.

## Authority Signals

- Add real author attribution where possible.
- Show medical review dates and reviewer names on medical-education content.
- Collect legitimate mentions from local press, NGOs, educational institutions, and medical communities.
- Encourage hospitals or experts featured on the platform to reference their listing or article page.

## Measurement

- Monitor Google Search Console for impressions, CTR, coverage, and rich result issues.
- Track top landing pages, branded queries, and city-specific queries in analytics.
- Review pages with high impressions and low CTR first, then improve title and description copy.
- Refresh underperforming pages with stronger content depth, better internal links, and clearer intent matching.
